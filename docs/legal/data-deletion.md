# Delete a Runcast account

In the iOS app, open **Account & Connections → Account control → Delete account** and confirm. Runcast attempts to revoke every linked Strava and Apple credential, then permanently deletes identities, preferences, saved routes and forecasts, watches and recommendations, sessions, device installations, and notification delivery records. The app removes SecureStore credentials and its user-scoped SQLite cache after the server confirms deletion.

If the in-app flow cannot be used, contact support@runcast.app from an email associated with the account. Support must verify ownership without asking for a token or password.
