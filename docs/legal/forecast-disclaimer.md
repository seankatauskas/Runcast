# Forecast disclaimer

Runcast recommendations are estimates based on third-party forecast data, mapped route geometry, pace assumptions, and a simplified comfort model. Numeric canopy data provides estimated filtering of direct solar radiation while diffuse radiation remains; it does not confirm geometric shade from individual trees, buildings, or terrain. Missing canopy data receives no shade reduction. Recommendations are not a guarantee of safe conditions and do not replace official weather alerts, local guidance, personal judgment, or medical advice.

Conditions can change quickly. Check official warnings, use appropriate visibility and hydration precautions, and do not run when lightning, flooding, extreme heat, poor air quality, ice, unsafe access, or other hazards are present. A “best” start means only the highest-scoring candidate evaluated within the selected window.
